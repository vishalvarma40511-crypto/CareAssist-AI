# 📖 Overview

Healthcare information is often confusing, fragmented, and difficult to access quickly. Patients spend hours searching the internet, waiting for appointments, or struggling to understand symptoms.

**CareAssist AI** solves this problem by combining Artificial Intelligence with modern healthcare services to create a smart digital healthcare assistant that helps users make informed decisions while encouraging consultation with qualified healthcare professionals.

Rather than replacing doctors, CareAssist AI acts as an intelligent companion that supports patients throughout their healthcare journey—from understanding symptoms to managing medications and booking appointments.
